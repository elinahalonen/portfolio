Academic Background

Three MSc degrees spanning marketing, linguistics, and cross-cultural psychology, plus time as a PhD candidate at the University of Turku.

Degrees

2015–2016 · Brunel University London, UK

MSc Cross-Cultural Psychology

Modules included foundations of cross-cultural psychology, evolutionary psychology, and research methods and statistics for psychology.

Dissertation: "Understanding the effect of subjective time perception on implicit reaction time methodologies."

2009–2010 · King's College London, UK

MSc Language, Discourse & Communication

European Social Research Council accredited researcher training, with modules in anthropological linguistics and social psychology in cross-cultural contexts (the latter taken at the London School of Economics).

Researcher training covered qualitative and quantitative methods (advanced statistics), research planning and management, and theories of social science.

Dissertation: 'Mediating measurement and meaning' — the challenges of translating questionnaires in multi-country research projects, examined from the translator's perspective using interviews, focus groups, and quantitative online surveys.

2000–2003, 2005–2006 · Turku Business School, Finland

BSc & MSc Marketing and Business Administration

In Finland, BSc and MSc are typically completed as a single degree. Studies covered marketing, advertising and corporate communication, European languages and intercultural communication, research methods and statistics, and foundations in business law and accounting.

Dissertation: Celebrity endorsement as a form of brand alliance. Case: Jamie Oliver and Sainsbury's Supermarkets Ltd. Published in a peer-reviewed journal with 264 citations and 24,000 downloads since 2010.

Includes a second-year exchange at Leeds Metropolitan University (BA Hons Public Relations) and a one-year internship at Sainsbury's Supermarkets as a public relations assistant.

PhD candidacy

I was briefly a PhD candidate at the University of Turku, during which time I published two peer-reviewed journal articles and presented at several international academic conferences. See publications for the full list.

Current studies

I am currently completing an MSc in AI & Governance at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam (Public Administration track). My thesis focuses on human oversight and delegation in agentic AI systems in the public sector. See thesis for more detail.
